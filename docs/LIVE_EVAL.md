### Longest pole, side by side

- **GitHub.** Live: Organization admin approval for private repository data access (if applicable).
  Library: Customer org owners. They must approve your OAuth App under Third-party Access (restrictions are on by default for new orgs) or install your GitHub App. GitHub itself has no review queue.
- **Gmail.** Live: Google OAuth restricted scope verification and third-party CASA security assessment queues.
  Library: Restricted-scope review by Google's Trust & Safety team, then the CASA assessment (lab scan, fixing findings, Letter of Validation). Much of the delay is waiting for Google's emails.
- **Shopify.** Live: Shopify app review process and automated quality checks.
  Library: The Shopify App Store review queue. Shopify states no timeline, and 2026 forum reports show weeks to more than two months stuck at "assigning a reviewer". A required fix can send you back into the queue.
- **Slack.** Live: Slack Marketplace functional review process (up to 10 weeks)
  Library: Slack Marketplace review: getting to 10+ active workspaces first, then up to 10 weeks of functional review. Resubmits during the preliminary stage reset your queue position.
- **Spotify.** Live: Meeting Spotify's strict Extended Quota Mode prerequisites (250k+ MAUs) and clearing corporate review.
  Library: Extended quota mode: first the Partner Application must be approved (no published timeline), then a quota-extension review that Spotify says can take up to six weeks. Only organisations with 250k+ MAU are eligible.
- **Webflow.** Live: App review and approval for marketplace or public workspace access.
  Library: Marketplace review: preparing the submission (testing site, demo video with OAuth approve/deny, policies, token attestations), then a decision Webflow aims to send within 10 business days. Each change request is another full round.
- **Xero.** Live: Onboarding 10 active beta customer connections and completing the security assessment for app certification.
  Library: App certification for the Plus tier. First you must get about ten real customer organisations connected on Core, which is capped at 50. Then Xero reviews the integration against its certification checkpoints and gives no published turnaround.
