## Independent verification

10 of 10 guides were re-checked by a separate verifier agent (a different model with a fresh context and an adversarial brief). It re-read the cited sources for the most decision-critical claims and re-ran probes. **90 claims checked: 53 confirmed, 24 corrected (and fixed in the guide), 13 unverifiable.**

### What was corrected

- **X (formerly Twitter) API**: New developers get pay-per-use only; no free tier; Public Utility apps get free access by approval — X's changelog (S7) only says pay-per-use launched Feb 6, 2026 and Basic/Pro remain for existing subscribers. 'No free tier' comes only from third parties (S19). Guide text now says so; open question added.
- **X (formerly Twitter) API**: Console path: User authentication settings with Read and write, Web App/Bot, exact callback (max 10), secrets shown once — S9 confirms confidential app types, exact-match callbacks (max 10) and show-once secrets. It names the DM permission 'Read, write, and DMs', not 'Read and write and Direct message'; fixed. Console layout can't be checked without login.
- **Microsoft OneNote, Word & To Do (Microsoft Graph)**: timeToProduction 1–3 weeks: Partner Center verification (3–5 business days) plus customer-tenant consent — Microsoft's 3–5 business days is confirmed, but 2026 community reports show 10+ days pending and over a month before approval. Widened to 1–6 weeks and fixed minHours (72 → 168) to match the label.
- **Microsoft OneNote, Word & To Do (Microsoft Graph)**: review_required = yes: org users can't consent to unverified multitenant apps (AADSTS900941 'risky') — Kept 'yes'. Step-up is on by default but only bites where user consent is enabled, for post-Nov-2020 multitenant apps asking beyond basic scopes, and admins can still consent. Detail tightened; AADSTS90094 added.
- **Microsoft OneNote, Word & To Do (Microsoft Graph)**: Tasks.* scopes have been excluded from user consent by Microsoft's default policy since June–July 2026 — Confirmed (S12 list; MC1304287 rollout June–July 2026) but only for tenants on the Microsoft-managed policy; custom policies are unaffected, and Files.ReadWrite.All isn't part of that change. Reworded the 'most tenants' claims.
- **Microsoft OneNote, Word & To Do (Microsoft Graph)**: Graph flags: Notes.ReadWrite, Files.ReadWrite and Tasks.ReadWrite need no admin consent and work for personal accounts — Permissions reference confirms both flags; Notes.ReadWrite.All and Bookings.* lack the personal-account marker. Fixed the Files.ReadWrite note: for personal accounts it also covers files shared with the user.
- **DocuSign (eSignature REST API)**: Go-Live results: Pending approval, or Declined + Verification required; manual reviews take 24–48 h — The platform page puts "manual reviews ... 24–48 hours" and the Verification link under Declined; Pending approval has no stated time. Rewrote step s11 to match; the Oct 2025 post says reviews take 48 h or less.
- **DocuSign (eSignature REST API)**: Console path: Admin → Integrations → Apps and Keys → add app; User Application Yes; Add Secret Key; Add URI; Save — Docusign's configure-app page confirms the secret, User Application, redirect URI, Save, 5-minute propagation and no-delete steps. It labels the add-app dialog button "ADD", not "Create App"; step s2 now notes both.
- **DocuSign (eSignature REST API)**: Polling: 1 GET status per envelope per 15 min; 3,000 calls/h; burst 200 demo/500 prod; ≤5 calls per envelope create/update — The limits page confirms the 15-minute rule (poll at 20), 3,000/h and the 200/500 burst. It has no "5 calls per envelope" rule, so I removed it from step s7 and the polling blocker; per-envelope GET and PUT limits remain.
- **Workday**: There is no self-serve route to a Workday tenant; tenant and sandbox access only through a formal partnership — Workday's Oct 2026 Developer Program lets individuals request 1 personal dev tenant (open beta, sample data, 7-day idle expiry). Rewrote headline, summary, cost, factors, steps, blocker. Difficulty stays gated via the customer-tenant gate.
- **Meta Ads (Marketing API)**: timeToProduction 2–10 weeks: Business Verification ~2 business days, App Review 'within a week', plus reports of longer waits — Stated times and report ranges check out, but minHours 240 (~10 days) contradicted the '2–10 weeks' label and a 3.5-month verification case exceeds the range. minHours → 336; basis now flags the outlier.
- **Meta Ads (Marketing API)**: Tech Provider Access Verification was retired on Oct 9, 2026 — The Oct 9 post says Access Verification 'will no longer be required' and its dashboard form 'will be removed', with no effective date. Reworded factor and nuance from 'retired' to 'announced'. Business Verification and App Review remain.
- **Meta Ads (Marketing API)**: Add the Composio callback under Facebook Login for Business → Settings → Valid OAuth Redirect URIs — Meta's security page confirms exact match and Strict Mode but gives no menu path; Composio says App Settings → Advanced.
