### Longest pole, side by side

- **Slack.** Live: Workspace admin approval gates or disabling restrictive app management settings in target workspaces.
  Library: Slack Marketplace review: getting to 10+ active workspaces first, then up to 10 weeks of functional review. Resubmits during the preliminary stage reset your queue position.
- **Spotify.** Live: Meeting strict business and MAU prerequisites alongside formal application review windows.
  Library: Extended quota mode: first the Partner Application must be approved (no published timeline), then a quota-extension review that Spotify says can take up to six weeks. Only organisations with 250k+ MAU are eligible.
- **Xero.** Live: Onboarding the required 10 active beta customers and completing initial security assessments for app certification.
  Library: App certification for the Plus tier. First you must get about ten real customer organisations connected on Core, which is capped at 50. Then Xero reviews the integration against its certification checkpoints and gives no published turnaround.
